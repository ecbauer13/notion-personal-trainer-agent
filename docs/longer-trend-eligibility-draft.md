# Longer-trend eligibility — approved rules

Approved by the user on 2026-10-11. Implemented, tested, and published on 2026-10-11. These are reporting safeguards chosen for this tracker, not physiological thresholds.

## Scope

Extend workout-performance assessment only. Existing body-weight maintenance/drift and monthly circumference rules remain separate.

Determine eligibility independently for each exercise, session group, and training block. Different blocks cannot supply one another's evidence. A block shorter than eight weeks will not qualify under this proposal; supporting cross-block assessment would require separately approved comparability rules.

## Evidence requirements

| Requirement | 8-week assessment | 12-week assessment |
|---|---|---|
| Recorded history age | At least eight inclusive weeks since the group's first valid recorded session | At least twelve inclusive weeks since the group's first valid recorded session |
| Rolling window | Latest eight weeks, inclusive of review end | Latest twelve weeks, inclusive of review end |
| Minimum usable sessions | Six sessions on distinct dates | Nine sessions on distinct dates |
| Distribution | Usable sessions in at least six of the eight anchored seven-day periods | Usable sessions in at least nine of the twelve anchored seven-day periods |
| Early evidence | At least one usable session in the window's first two weeks | At least one usable session in the window's first two weeks |
| Current evidence | At least one usable session in the latest week | At least one usable session in the latest week |

Implementation dates: eight weeks means 56 inclusive dates (start = end minus 55 days); twelve weeks means 84 inclusive dates (start = end minus 83 days). Seven-day periods are anchored backward from the review end in America/New_York. History age uses the first valid recorded session in the same group, not workflow creation date or the user's inferred training experience. Use only week labels in the report.

Usable sessions require the existing valid working-set loads/reps and unambiguous exercise/session/block identity. Repeated rows on one date cannot inflate evidence. Existing invalid-data and duplicate-date rules apply; do not silently discard unresolved evidence within a group's window and claim a clean trend. Changed set count remains a mixed comparison under the approved rules, not automatic improvement or an excuse to omit the session.

## Gaps and output

Missing workout weeks are neutral. The distribution requirement permits limited gaps, but assesses only recorded sessions and does not establish continuous progress through unobserved periods. No imputation, zero filling, assumed regression, or instruction to catch up.

Do not show an 8- or 12-week assessment before that window qualifies. Suppress empty longer-trend sections and countdowns. Eligibility makes assessment possible; it does not guarantee a finding worth reporting. Any eventual finding must add a distinct useful observation rather than repeat the weekly analysis.

Body-weight vacation gaps continue to break its consecutive sufficiently tracked sequence. Workout eligibility does not relax that rule.

## Why these requirements

Requiring observations in roughly three quarters of the window balances coverage with occasional missed logging or vacation. Early and current evidence prevent a recent cluster from being labeled as a longer assessment. These approved thresholds are adjustable product decisions. Classification and reporting rules have also been approved, implemented, and published.
